Turn a bass recording into tabs

Drop audio files here (WAV, MP3, M4A…) or choose them below. Pick several stems at once (Ctrl/⌘ or Shift + click) (bass, drums, guitar…) to practise along with the whole song. The tab is made from the track with "bass" in its name.
To reopen a saved project, choose the .basstab file together with its audio files.
Everything runs locally in your browser.

Note detection (re-runs and resets edits)

How easily a new note start (pluck) is detected. Raise it if repeated notes on the same fret merge into one. Lower it if a single note gets split into several (for example from string noise or fret buzz). At 0, only very strong attacks count.

Notes shorter than this are thrown away. Raise it to remove short stray blips and ghost notes. Lower it for fast passages, where real 16th notes are going missing.

How clearly pitched the sound must be to count as a note (1 = a perfectly clean tone). Raise it to cut wrong or noisy notes. Lower it if real notes are missing, for example on heavily distorted or effected bass.

Sounds quieter than this (relative to the loudest part) are ignored. Raise it (towards −10) for noisy recordings, or to stop notes ringing on too long. Lower it if soft notes disappear.

Note split in two? Lower Onset sensitivity first. You can also select the second half and press Merge with previous (key J).
